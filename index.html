💡 Good to Know

💰 Payments

Swiss Franc (CHF) widely accepted. Euros also accepted near borders. Credit cards accepted in most hotels and restaurants.

🚌 Getting Around

Coaches and trams included per itinerary. Swiss trains are punctual and scenic — GoldenPass is a must-see highlight!

📶 Internet / Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi in most hotels. Consider an international data plan or Swiss SIM for constant connectivity.

🌤️ Weather — July

Warm: 18–28°C in lowlands. Cooler in mountains: 10–20°C. Pack layers and rain gear for Alpine days.

🍽️ Meals

Lunch and dinner not included. Swiss cuisine: fondue, raclette, rösti. Book popular restaurants in advance.

💶 Tipping

Service charge included in Switzerland. A small tip (5–10%) for exceptional service is appreciated.

⚡ Electricity

Type J plug (3-pin). Same voltage as North America (230V). Pack a universal adapter.

🗣️ Language

Four national languages: German, French, Italian, Romansh. You'll encounter German (Zurich, Bern) and French (Geneva, Montreux).
